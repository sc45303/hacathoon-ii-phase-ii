# Design & Theme Skill

## Instructions

1. **Color palette**

   - Primary, secondary, accent colors
   - Background, card, and text contrast

2. **Typography**

   - Standardized font sizes and line heights
   - Headings, body text, buttons styled consistently

3. **Visual hierarchy**
   - Consistent spacing and alignment
   - Shadows, borders, and rounded corners

## Best Practices

- Maintain accessible contrast ratios
- Use modular theme classes
- Ensure design consistency across pages
